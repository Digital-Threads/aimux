Pace. A window's use so far is carried to its reset at the same rate. Tight means on course for 85% or more, over pace means it runs out before the reset, spent means it is full now. For the weekly window the forecast counts twelve working hours to a day, not twenty-four. There is no forecast in the first minutes of a window.

unknown and unknown (codex) are sessions aimux could not tie to a profile: started outside aimux, or before it was installed.

Not counted in usage: sessions that hooks and plugins run on their own in the background. They do use the limits, so a limit can fill faster than the usage here suggests.
